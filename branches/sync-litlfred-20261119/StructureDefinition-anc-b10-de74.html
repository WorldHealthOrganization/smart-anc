Resource Profile: Severe hypertension

Official URL: http://smart.who.int/anc/StructureDefinition/anc-b10-de74 Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-19 Computable Name: Severehypertension

Usage:Workflow Task: Diagnosis & treatment

Whether the client has developed severe hypertension associated with pregnancy, as defined in the decision logic (If SBP is 160 mmHg or higher and/or DBP is 110 mmHg or higher)
